About us

Aroha IAS

At Aroha IAS Academy, we believe that UPSC preparation becomes meaningful when it is simple, well-structured, and student-friendly. Built on the guidance of experienced mentors who have appeared multiple times for the UPSC interview, Aroha aims to support aspirants with clarity, consistency, and a clear sense of direction. Whether you are beginning your journey or refining your approach, we strive to make your preparation more focused, manageable, and motivating—so that you can move forward with confidence at every step.

Interactive Online Classes

Engage in live, interactive sessions designed for flexible learning, allowing you to study at your own pace from anywhere in the world.

Comprehensive Student Support

Receive ongoing guidance, mentorship, and access to resources throughout your UPSC preparation journey to ensure consistent progress.
